Tips & Tricks

Essential advice for new players starting in the Living Lands.

🎯 Combat Tips

  • • Practice perfect timing - Dodge and block at the right moment
  • • Use terrain - Funnel enemies through narrow passages
  • • Manage stamina - Don't spam attacks
  • • Switch targets - Focus fire weakest enemies first

🗺️ Exploration Tips

  • • Search everywhere - Side paths often have loot
  • • Talk to NPCs - They give hints and side quests
  • • Check your map - Mark unexplored areas
  • • Rest at camps - Save progress and manage items

📈 Progression Tips

  • • Focus on one class - Start simple, multiclass later
  • • Upgrade weapons - Better gear makes a huge difference
  • • Complete side quests - Better XP than grinding
  • • Choose backgrounds wisely - Match your playstyle

💡 General Tips

  • • Fighter is best for beginners - Tanky and forgiving
  • • Save often - Multiple save slots recommended
  • • Read item descriptions - Lore and hints hidden inside
  • • Experiment with builds - Test different abilities
