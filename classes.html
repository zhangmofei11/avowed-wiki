Classes Guide

Choose your class wisely. Each has unique abilities and playstyles.

⚔️

Fighter

S-Tier

Tanky melee warrior. High survivability with strong close-combat damage. Best class for beginners.

Starting Background

War Hero - boosts Might & Resolve

Core Abilities

  • • Heavy melee weapons
  • • Shield blocking
  • • Stamina management
  • • Crowd control
🏹

Ranger

B-Tier

Versatile hybrid. Mix of ranged attacks and stealth mechanics. Great for exploration.

Starting Background

Vanguard Scout - boosts Perception, Constitution, Dexterity

Core Abilities

  • • Bow & arrow
  • • Stealth mechanics
  • • Trap setting
  • • Pet companion
🔮

Wizard

A-Tier

Highest damage potential. Powerful elemental spells. Requires strategic positioning.

Starting Background

Court Sorcerer - boosts Perception & Intellect

Core Abilities

  • • Fire, Ice, Shock spells
  • • Arcane weapons
  • • AOE attacks
  • • Multi-classing

Multi-classing

Avowed supports multi-classing, allowing you to combine abilities from different classes for unique builds.
